# Usage Rights Rules

Legal rules for the use of digital assets.

## Usage rights period
usageRightsStart is the first and usageRightsEnd the last day on which an asset may be used. usageRightsStart must
not be after usageRightsEnd. Assets with expired usage rights must not be published and are flagged as errors by
validation; assets whose rights expire within 30 days produce a warning so that renewals can be negotiated.

## Who may change usage rights
Usage rights are contractual facts maintained by the rights management team. They are never inferred or changed by
AI. Changes to usage rights are always made manually and are recorded in the audit history.

## Copyright ownership
copyrightOwner names the legal copyright holder, for example Sample Apparel for in-house productions or the agency
for licensed images. An embedded copyright notice in EXIF or XMP metadata is useful evidence, but copyright
ownership is never changed automatically: AI proposals for copyrightOwner always require human approval.

## Markets
market lists the markets (GLOBAL, EU, US, UK, APAC, NORDICS) in which an asset may be published according to its
licence. It is maintained by the rights management team and never set by AI.
