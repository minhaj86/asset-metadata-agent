# Taxonomy Guidelines

How assets are classified in the hierarchical product taxonomy.

## Structure
The taxonomy has three top-level branches: Fashion, Home and Marketing. Fashion is split by department
(Men, Women, Kids), then by group (Clothing, Shoes, Accessories) and finally by product type, for example
Fashion > Men > Clothing > Shirts or Fashion > Women > Clothing > Dresses.

## Classification rules
Always classify to the most specific existing node (a leaf such as Shirts, not Clothing).
The department must match the product information system: a men's shirt belongs under Fashion > Men.
Never create or modify taxonomy nodes during classification; nodes are maintained by the taxonomy stewards.
If no existing node fits, leave taxonomyPath empty and flag the asset for the taxonomy stewards.

## Campaign and editorial content
Campaign hero images and lookbook material are classified under Marketing > Campaigns, for example
Marketing > Campaigns > Seasonal Campaigns. Product images used in campaigns keep their product classification.

## Approval
Taxonomy changes affect navigation and reporting and therefore always require human approval, even when the
classification confidence is high.
