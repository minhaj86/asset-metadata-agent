# Photography and Alt Text Guidelines

Guidelines for product photography and for writing accessible and searchable descriptions.

## Product packshots
Standard product images are STUDIO shots on a WHITE background with the product facing the camera (FRONT).
Every product needs at least a FRONT and a BACK view; DETAIL shots show fabric, collar or buttons.

## Alt text guidelines
Alt text describes what is visible for people using screen readers. Write one sentence of 15 to 250 characters.
Start with the view, then the colour, the target department and the product, then the background, for example:
"Front view of a light blue men's Oxford shirt on a white background".
Do not start with "Image of" or "Picture of", do not repeat the filename and do not add marketing claims.

## Title guidelines
Titles are short and factual: product name plus view, for example "Regular Fit Oxford Shirt - Front View".
Maximum 120 characters.

## Keyword guidelines
Use five to ten lowercase keywords: product type, distinctive product name words, colour, department
(menswear, womenswear, kidswear) and material, for example shirt, oxford, blue, menswear, cotton.
Avoid generic words (image, photo, new, sale) and keywords that contradict the colour or product type.

## Description guidelines
Descriptions state the shot type, product, colour and material, for example
"Studio image of the Regular Fit Oxford Shirt in light blue cotton, shown from the front."
