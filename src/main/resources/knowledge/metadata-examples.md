# Metadata Examples

Reference examples of complete and incomplete metadata.

## Complete PRODUCT_IMAGE example
productId 12345, productType SHIRT, color BLUE, angleFacing FRONT, shotType STUDIO, backgroundType WHITE,
material COTTON, copyrightOwner Sample Apparel, usageRightsEnd 2027-12-31,
title "Regular Fit Oxford Shirt - Front View",
altText "Front view of a light blue men's Oxford shirt on a white background",
keywords shirt, oxford, blue, menswear, cotton, taxonomyPath Fashion > Men > Clothing > Shirts.

## Complete CAMPAIGN_IMAGE example
campaign SUMMER_2026, season SUMMER, market EU and US, copyrightOwner Sample Apparel, usageRightsStart 2026-05-01,
usageRightsEnd 2026-10-31, altText "Group of models in colourful summer outfits on a sunny beach",
intendedForABTest true with experimentIdentifier EXP-2026-014.

## Typical problems
A product image without copyrightOwner cannot be published. Alt text such as "Image of shirt" is too short and
starts with a redundant phrase. Keywords such as "new" or "sale" are irrelevant. A shirt classified under
Fashion > Women > Clothing > Dresses is an inconsistent classification.
